Print Kit

At Staples, ask for actual size / 100% — no fit-to-page. Every PDF is US Letter with the sizes baked in. Everything that gets printed, sorted into six Staples orders: five document orders by paper, and one photo print. Order 6, the combat cheat sheet, is a small order on its own. Each file is one upload, one copy. Cut at home afterwards. Every page is previewed below — tap one to see it full size.

Before you print

How Staples works

Online, then pick up (easiest): staples.com → Print & Marketing Services → Copies & Documents. Upload the PDF, choose the options below, pick your store. Order by 12 pm for same-day pickup; later orders are next day, and busy periods can push same-day items to next day.

Or walk in with the PDFs on a USB stick (or emailed to yourself) and ask the copy center for the same settings.

Documents print on 8.5×11, 8.5×14 or 11×17. Cardstock and gloss are among the premium paper options, and cutting is offered as a finishing option. The site doesn't list exact cardstock weights or cutting prices, so ask for the heaviest cardstock they have and get the price at the counter. Every file is a PDF, which is what they recommend.

Sources: Staples Document & Copy Printing · Staples Printing & Marketing Services · Staples same-day services

Rebuilding the PDFs

From the repo root, with Google Chrome installed:

python3 print/build.py

The invitations and the poster are Spencer's art (src/assets/spencer-*.jpg), laid out by src/invite.html and src/poster.html. That also remakes photo-4x6.jpg from photo-4x6.html (needs ImageMagick); --photo-only remakes just the photo, and --combat-only just the combat cheat sheet.

The tickets print their own show details; --band, --venue, --city, --date and --time override them. Each prop's own print layout decides how it looks, so fix the prop, not the script. Re-run after any prop change, and commit the new PDFs and JPG.
