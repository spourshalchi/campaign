Dood Mansion

A campy, early-2000s young-adult vampire mystery. Super-seniors at a fantasy high school in Transyltown, NY get mysterious invites to the abandoned Dutch mansion on the edge of town — and walk straight into a very obvious trap. (Dood = "dead" in Dutch.)

Tone: I Know What You Did Last Summer, but DnD Format: One weekend · 2 sessions Twist: the vampires are the good guys Big Bad: DJ Poe Boy

    How the weekend unfolds

    A haunted-house structure: curiosity gets them in, the doors bolt shut, and everything after plays out inside Dood Mansion — the fork is cut, so it now runs one line from the locker cards to the Volvo in the driveway.

    Between beats is a transition — a line to say at the table to move them to the next one, plus what to have ready underneath it. Transitions aren’t tracked as canon: they’re table patter, not lore, so say them however you like or ignore them. A gold dashed transition means there’s still a design question open there. The numbered beats themselves are settled.

    Combat encounters

    Three fights for the weekend, one held in reserve. The beats are settled; every number below — XP budgets, CR picks, HP, stat-block chassis, and the contents of Saturday's fight — is a proposal built against them and agreed to by nobody. Combat and rules are Scott's lane.
