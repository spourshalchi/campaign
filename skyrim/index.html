The Storm Adept

An elemental mage who fights with runes and two-element combos, one element per hand, aimed separately with SpellAim. When Magicka runs dry or enemies get too close, one Bind switches you to Spellsiphon: siphon, Ward and recover, then go back to casting. Every number was read from the plugins in Lost Legacy 2.0.2 plus Spellsiphon 5.28.

Get these, in this order

  1. Spellsiphoning book: Sleeping Giant Inn bar, Riverwood (free). Your backup mode.
  2. Cheese and pastries: Riverwood Trader or the inn. A regen % buff before every fight.
  3. Draining Touch: Farengar, Whiterun. Drains Health and Magicka. Confirmed in his stock.
  4. Fire Blast, Ice Lance or Stormstrike (Forgotten Magic, Destruction 25): Elethor at the Bannered Mare, Whiterun. They grow stronger with use. Confirmed.
  5. One fire and one frost spell until then: Farengar (Apocalypse Blaze and Hailstone if he has them), or keep your class's starting spells.
  6. Wild Healing (Apocalypse, Novice Restoration): any court mage or Colette at the College.
  7. Julianos: sneak and press A on his shrine at the Temple of the Divines, Solitude.
  8. Solar Ray or Holy Fire: Rorlund, the same temple. Confirmed.
  9. Gourmet recipes: Riverwood Trader, Belethor or Carlotta (Whiterun), Odar (Solitude). Fondue is the best, and appears in the higher-tier recipe stock.
  10. Apprentice Stone: Hjaalmarch marshes near Morthal, once you're comfortable.
  11. Step Through Shadows: Enthir at the College (Alteration 25). Confirmed.
  12. Ocato's Recital: Tolfdir at the College (Alteration 25).
  13. Adept spells (Bolide, Ice Shiv): Faralda, at Destruction 50.

"Confirmed" means it's in that merchant's stock in the mod files. Apocalypse tomes are added to every vanilla spell vendor's lists, so they can turn up at any of them; stock is random by day and level. The Spellsiphon book (Sleeping Giant Inn) and the Julianos shrine (Temple of the Divines) are verified in the files.

Backstory: Caelinwe, the Runaway Sapiarch

Caelinwe is a High Elf (Altmer) from the Summerset Isles. She trained under the Sapiarchs, Summerset's master scholars. Like every Altmer mage she was taught to honour Magnus, the god of magic, and Auri-El, the elves' name for the dragon god Akatosh.

Summerset is now run by the Thalmor, the elf-supremacist government of the Aldmeri Dominion. After the Great War against the Empire, the Thalmor forced a treaty (the White-Gold Concordat) that banned the worship of Talos. Their Justiciars now hunt Talos worshippers across Skyrim. Caelinwe saw those she studied with recruited into that work, and she left. A Sapiarch-trained mage walking out on the Dominion is a deserter as far as the Thalmor are concerned.

She crossed into Skyrim in the middle of its civil war and took a room in Helgen under a plain name. That's where the game finds her (Alternate Perspective's inn start).

In Riverwood she finds a treatise on Spellsiphoning (the mod's own lore): an obscure art of drawing magic from the world instead of yourself. Nobody taught it on Summerset, which is exactly why she wants it.

Hooks in the game

  • Julianos is the Nine Divines' god of wisdom, logic and magic, and patron of mages. For an Altmer, choosing an Imperial Divine over Magnus is a quiet act of rebellion. She follows him at the Temple of the Divines in Solitude.
  • The Thalmor: she avoids or opposes them every time. Diplomatic Immunity (the main quest's Thalmor Embassy party) is personal.
  • The College of Winterhold takes in mages of any race, though Nords distrust it after the Great Collapse. It's the one place she belongs.
  • The Psijic Order is an ancient order of mages from the Isle of Artaeum, older than the Thalmor, who answer to no government. Joining them (the Isle of Artaeum mod, after the College questline) is her endgame.
  • The Dragonborn Gallery: she hands Auryen the relics the Thalmor would want.
  • The Crown at Destruction 100: Storms. It halves fire and frost, so she ends as a pure storm mage.

Lore in 60 seconds

  • Skyrim is at war with itself. The Stormcloaks (Ulfric, Windhelm) want independence and Talos back. The Imperial Legion (General Tullius, Solitude) holds the Empire together. The Thalmor quietly want both sides worn down.
  • Nords distrust elves, and High Elves most of all, because they look like Thalmor. Expect "knife-ear" comments, especially in Windhelm, the Stormcloak capital.
  • Nords distrust mages too. Many blame the College for the collapse of Winterhold, the city it sits in.
  • The Nine Divines: Akatosh, Arkay, Dibella, Julianos, Kynareth, Mara, Stendarr, Zenithar and Talos, the human hero-god the Thalmor banned. Daedric Princes such as Azura and Sanguine are powerful, older spirits outside the Divines.
  • You are Dragonborn: born with a dragon's soul, so you can absorb dragon souls and learn shouts. The Greybeards of High Hrothgar teach the Voice.

How Caelinwe acts

  • Quiet and careful. She doesn't announce where she's from. She reads every book (which fits Experience and Avid Reader), and asks before she fights.
  • Never helps the Thalmor. She warns Talos worshippers they're being watched, and helps anyone hiding from Justiciars.
  • In the civil war she leans Imperial. The Stormcloaks distrust elves, but she sees that a broken Empire helps the Thalmor most. Staying neutral is also in character.
  • Curious about forbidden magic (Spellsiphon, Undeath, Daedric artifacts), but she turns down anything that hurts innocents.
  • Wary of Daedra: she bargains with them, but doesn't worship them.

Playing Caelinwe

Her arc in four acts

  1. Hiding (Helgen → Whiterun): wear a hood or circlet, keep to inns and roads, and give a fake name if asked. The less anyone knows, the better.
  2. Belonging (the College): the first place she drops the act. She shares what she knows and argues theory with the scholars.
  3. Defiance (Thalmor Embassy, Markarth): once she's strong enough, she stops hiding and starts working against the Thalmor.
  4. Transcendence (Psijics, Crown of Storms): she becomes something Summerset can't claim.

Daily habits

  • Pray to Julianos every day (Wintersun's Pray power). Her one ritual.
  • Read every book she finds, ideally by a campfire at night. It gives XP too (Experience, Avid Reader).
  • Prepare before every fight: eat something (she's learning to like Nord cheese), cast armor, lay runes. A Sapiarch never walks in unprepared.
  • Treat Spellsiphon as a guilty secret. Use it only when cornered, and never in front of College staff until she trusts them.

How she talks

  • Polite and precise. She persuades rather than threatens, and pays rather than steals.
  • When Nords call her "knife-ear", she lets it go. They aren't wrong to fear her people.
  • She never says she's Thalmor, and if asked she says plainly that she isn't.

Choices she'd make

  • Whiterun: she stops to listen to Heimskr preaching Talos. Banned faith interests her, and she'd tip him.
  • Thalmor patrols on the roads (with a prisoner): she frees the prisoner.
  • Markarth: she distrusts Ondolemar, the Thalmor Justiciar, on sight and refuses his errands.
  • Civil war: she sides with the Imperials, or stays out of it.
  • Paarthurnax: spares him. Knowledge that old shouldn't be destroyed.
  • Azura's Star: returns it to Azura's priestess, not the black-soul version. She won't trap people's souls.
  • Dark Brotherhood: destroys them (and joins the Penitus Oculatus mod's follow-up).
  • Thieves Guild: passes. Stealing doesn't fit her.
  • Companions: respects them, but they're a Nord warrior hall, not her place.
  • Daedric quests: takes them only when the Prince's price doesn't harm innocents. She'll bargain, but never bow.

Company she'd keep

  • Lucien (Dead Man's Drink, Falkreath): a fellow scholar. The best fit.
  • Inigo (Riften jail): another outsider Skyrim distrusts.
  • Aviendha (outside Riverwood): a warrior with her own exile story.

Why this pairing works

  • Spellsiphon unlocks need your base Destruction or Conjuration at 10/30/50/70. A Destruction main gets them naturally.
  • Destruction perks apply to Spellsiphon too: its Draw, World, Life and Death effects are Destruction-school in the plugin, and using them levels Destruction.
  • Regen % (MagickaRateMult) helps both halves: it refills your Magicka and multiplies Spellsiphon damage. Base regen (High Elf, Apprentice Stone) only refills Magicka, which is still great for a caster.
  • Woven Power from Spellsiphon raises regen %, so your backup mode refuels your main mode.

Character creation

Race
High Elf: +50 Magicka and 50% faster base regen. (Breton if you'd rather absorb spells.)
Class
Select Class book → custom. Ability: Potent (+50% regen %). Majors: Destruction, Alteration, Restoration, Conjuration, Enchanting, Illusion.
Birthsign
Lady: +25% Health and Magicka regen %.
Stone
Take Mage at the Guardian Stones first. Then switch to Apprentice (in the Hjaalmarch marshes near Morthal): double base regen, but 25% weaker to magic. Spellsiphon's Ward blocks spells, which covers that weakness.
God
Julianos: spells cost less as favor grows, then get stronger at full devotion. Follow him by sneaking (left bottom side button) and pressing A on his shrine.

Avoid: the Atronach Stone (lowers regen %), Magnus (no regen), and Apprentice's Zeal on top of the Apprentice Stone (double magic weakness).

Main mode: Storm Adept

Hands: a fire spell in one, frost or shock in the other.

  1. Pre-fight: Ocato's Preparation auto-casts your best armor spell. In robes, Mage Armor makes it 2× to 3× stronger.
  2. Runes first: Rune Master gives 3× as many runes, 75 ft farther away. Trap the path they'll take.
  3. Combo: hit one target with two different elements, one per hand. Red Mage triggers combo effects (fire plus frost makes armor brittle), and Elementalist adds +5% per switch.
  4. Shock refills you: Static Drain (Destruction 50) absorbs Magicka per hit.

Backup mode: Spellsiphon

When: Magicka is low, an enemy reaches you, or a mage is pelting you.

  1. Bind (R bumper, or SpellAim's both-hands thrust gesture) puts Draw and Ward in your hands.
  2. Ward: blocks spells, cuts physical damage by 80% and heals. Time it to knock attackers back.
  3. Draw Life from the enemy, World from the ground and Death from a body. Each switch stacks Woven Power (+regen %), so your Magicka refills while you fight.
  4. Once you're topped up, equip your two element spells again (favorites or gestures) and return to main mode.

Don't Entangle your main spells: Entangle folds a spell into Draw and removes it from normal casting. Entangle only spare elemental spells, if any. Leave the Refocus power off too: it forces Draw and Ward back after every other spell.

Close-range option: Bind with no element held gives a bound sword. It's a real one-handed sword, and its damage scales with Health. Use it to finish off a weakened enemy. Discharge Life with it to stop time.

Perks (Vokriinator Black, the actual trees)

Levels 1–15

  • Destruction: Destruction Mastery. Spellsiphon's Draw, World, Life and Death are Destruction-school effects, so using them levels Destruction too.
  • Alteration: Alteration Mastery → Initiate's Charm (20) → Mage Armor (30).
  • Restoration: Restoration Mastery → Descending Light (20). A Magicka surge when combat starts.

Levels 15–30

  • Elementalist (Destruction 40)
  • Rune Master (Destruction 40)
  • Spell Surge (Destruction 35): cheaper dual casts
  • Catch Fire or Piercing Cold (35)
  • Ocato's Preparation (Alteration 40)
  • Fortification (Alteration 45): +100 armor in robes
  • Respite (Restoration 40): Spellsiphon's Ward checks for it and restores Stamina, so Ward breaks less often.
  • Inner Light (Restoration 30, rank 2 at 60): +1% (then 2%) of your total Magicka back per second.

Later

  • Static Drain (Destruction 50): shock absorbs Magicka
  • Red Mage (Destruction 60)
  • Witch's Familiar (Conjuration 50): +50% regen % and +15% spell power while your familiar is out
  • Augmented (one element at 80)
  • Rune Master 70 (any distance, +20%)
  • At Destruction 100, a Crown of Storms, Inferno or Winter: immune to that element, +35% to it, and a once-a-day storm, meteor or blizzard power. The other two elements become 50% weaker, so the two-element combo ends here: take Crown of Storms and go all-shock (Static Drain keeps refilling you).

Skip Vancian Magic (it stops casting until you rest) and Blood Mage.
