Running the house

  • Saturday: in the front door, the bolt throws, Angelique pulls them down in the hall. They hide under the dining-room table and overhear the vampires at the hearth (“have our meals been fed?”). Dracula drags them out; Orlok ends it. His spectacles are on the west sill of the same room, where the late light falls (cue 7).
  • Saturday, late: up the stair to the passage. The warm door is the sealed chamber (cue 8); the botched summoning fights in it and claws across the passage at the music-room door (cue 9).
  • Sunday, any order: the study (sealed door off the hall), the drawing room (the party room), the cellar (down from the kitchen). The music room upstairs stays locked until the Spirit opens it.
