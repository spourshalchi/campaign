## Cornelis Dood / the Gjenganger — original entry

**Status: not canon.** Scott parked the whole creature for a later pass. The doc-sourced parts
below are Spencer's (Sesh 2 and Sesh 6); everything else is an unreviewed proposal from a
codex session and should be treated as a first draft, not a spec.

### What's actually in the doc (Spencer)

- **Sesh 2** — found the Gjenganger during folklore research and offered it as *"an option for a
  ghost entity that could be the thing possessing the instant messaging chat."* Closed with
  *"Up to you if we go with it."* Never answered.
- **Sesh 2 folklore** — corporeal, not ghostly; died before its time; revenge-motivated against
  those who wronged it in life; attacks sleepers by pinching them, leaving a blue mark that
  signals coming sickness; touch makes flesh slough away; prevented by burying the at-risk
  person with a runic inscription inside the coffin.
- **Sesh 6** — reassigned it himself: fight it *"to get to the computer & interact with it,"*
  while the spirit in the computer becomes Poe Boy's victim. Advantage in close range, so the
  counterplay is distance. Backstory *"could be"* the original owner — never married, no
  children, isolated, died by suicide from loneliness; *"could be a big part of the reason it
  was abandoned, other than the rumors about the vamps."* A development beat for Bix.
- **Sesh 6, unused** — Bix's overnight account at the mansion *"could be incomplete"* and a
  **diary** in the house reveals something. Also: **real-estate scouting documents** as a
  findable clue.

### The unreviewed proposal

- Its job is to explain why the house is feared — what Bix and Gnomey each ran into as kids,
  and the reputation that let three vampires live there undisturbed.
- Its motive: everyone who wronged it is a century dead. Poe Boy puts a soul in a machine
  upstairs, and it finds the one thing in the house as alone as it is. It isn't guarding the
  computer, it's keeping the kid.
- Bix's overnight gap is the folklore's blue pinch-mark, carried for years unknowingly.
- The diary is Cornelis's — forty years alone, plus an entry about the boy asleep in his hall.
- Poe Boy's botched soul cage broke the burial rite, making Cornelis sworn to him.
- Resolution: reset the runic marker on the heath. A mercy, not a kill.
- Stat block draft: MM Revenant (CR 5) chassis at 120 HP for five level-4 PCs, plus a 10-ft
  necrotic aura to enforce ranged play, Regeneration suppressed by radiant damage or a
  marker fragment, Rejuvenation so killing him doesn't end him, and an "Anchored" trait so he
  holds the door rather than chasing.
- Encounter draft: the objective is the door, not the corpse. Narrow corridor limits engagement
  to two PCs. Ways through: kill him, draw him off, or reach him — Bix being the one he'd hear.
