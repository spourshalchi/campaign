Goblin · Circle of Wildfire Druid

Not everyone comes out on top, so you'll do whatever it takes not to get left behind. Some things have to burn so something better can grow.

What you remember

Gnomey Soprano

Julia

Gnome · Rogue

You look small, cute and easy to bully. People who know better don't cross you — you're petty, and you'll go to extravagant lengths to get even.

What you remember

  • You're a hustler who loves money — smart, just not into books. One teacher really believes in you, and you keep going back and forth between this life and getting out through college.
  • You sell cigarettes in the school bathroom, which ties you to Lil Stinky, the school kingpin. You know every back road and pumpkin patch in the county.
  • Your family is very wholesome. You stick out. You grew up idolizing mob bosses.
  • In middle school you ran with Poe Boy's crew. It ended over the silly bandz racket — he was skimming your cut, and you walked.
  • The crew used to throw rocks at the Dood mansion and dare each other to ring the doorbell. One Halloween, something happened up there.
  • Great Uncle Vinny ran cigarettes and liquor on the back roads, way back. He's ancient now, in a home, mostly gone. When you were twelve he drove over: Twelve years old and he's got a crew. I don't have a crew anymore. You'll get another one.
